## 3. The gap: demo AI → production AI

Demos forgive mistakes. Production doesn’t.

Without controls, agents can call the wrong tools, pull the wrong data, quietly break policy, and leave you with no trail to explain what happened.

The gap isn’t “smarter models.” It’s **bounds, approvals, evidence, and a product someone on your team can own after handoff.**

---

## 4. What you get

Four concrete deliverables — not a slide deck of buzzwords:

1. **Human-in-the-loop gates** — High-risk actions wait for a person. Low-risk steps can run; the line is explicit.
2. **Tool and data allowlists** — The agent only touches what you named. Everything else is out of reach by default.
3. **Audit logs and replay** — Prompts, tool calls, and decisions in a trail you can export and review.
4. **Policy + eval harness before ship** — Soak and eval against abuse cases (including prompt injection and data leakage) before it hits real users.

---

## 5. How an engagement runs

Builder process, not agency waterfall:

1. **Discover risks** — Where the AI sits, what it can touch, what “bad” looks like for your team.
2. **Threat and control map** — Gates, allowlists, logging, and ownership — written down before we scale the happy path.
3. **Build with guards** — Product + agent/workflows with controls in from sprint one, not bolted on after a scare.
4. **Prove with soak and evals** — Exercise failure modes; fix what breaks before you call it done.
5. **Handoff runbooks** — Who can change prompts, tools, and policy — and how you operate it without me in the loop.

## 8. FAQ

**Do you make us “EU AI Act compliant”?**  
No. I don’t sell certification. I design systems **toward** EU AI Act and NIST AI RMF *concepts* — oversight, transparency, bounded behavior, documentation — so your compliance process has something real to work with. Legal sign-off stays with you and your counsel.

**Can you work in our VPC / private cloud?**  
Yes — private deployment and least-privilege access are normal for this kind of work. Exact hosting is decided in scoping from your constraints.

**Who owns the model and the IP?**  
You do, unless we agree otherwise in writing. Prompts, policies, allowlists, and application code are yours at handoff.

**What won’t you automate?**  
Anything that can move money, change access, delete data, or contact customers without a human gate — unless you explicitly accept that risk in writing and we design for it. Default is: high-impact actions need a person.

**How is this different from hiring an AI agency?**  
Agencies often ship a demo and leave the product, controls, and ownership as your problem. I wrangle the AI **and** ship the product around it — one partner, controls in from the start, runbooks at handoff.

**What’s the timeline?**  
Depends on risk surface and integrations. Enterprise control work is scoped honestly; I don’t promise “1–2 week MVP” on this page. First call is for a realistic shape, not a fake date.

**What do you need from us to start?**  
A real use case, who owns risk internally, where the data lives, and which systems the agent must (and must not) touch. A sandbox helps; a blank “build us AI” brief doesn’t.

**Do you only do the model layer, or the product too?**  
Both. UI, workflows, integrations, and the agent bounds are one build. If you only want prompts with no product, I’m the wrong hire.

**How does handoff work?**  
You get the system, the control map, logs/eval setup, and a runbook for who changes what. Goal: your team can operate and extend it without me as a single point of failure.
